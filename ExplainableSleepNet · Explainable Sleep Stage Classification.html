Sleep staging you can actually trust

An explainable deep learning framework that classifies sleep stages from EEG signals and shows exactly which seconds and which frequency bands drove each decision.

The problem

Scoring one night of polysomnography takes up to three hours, and human experts disagree 18–25% of the time. Deep networks match experts, but their black-box output gives clinicians nothing to audit.

Multi-scale 1D-CNN

Parallel kernels (k=7 and k=31) capture fast spindles and slow delta waves together.

BiLSTM context

Forward and backward memory models dependencies across 46 sub-windows of each epoch.

Temporal self-attention

Learned weights αt pinpoint which seconds of the 30 s epoch drove the prediction.

STFT band attribution

Attention is projected onto spectrograms to give Delta, Theta, Alpha and Beta contributions.

Clinical narrative

Auto-generated, AASM-aligned text report ready for a medical chart entry.

Faithfulness tested

Top-k deletion tests show attention is causally linked to the output, not decoration.

Five-stage pipeline

From a raw 3-channel, 30-second epoch (Fpz-Cz, Pz-Oz, EOG at 100 Hz) to an auditable diagnosis.

1 · Acquire3 × 3000 samples per epoch
2 · FilterButterworth 0.5–40 Hz, zero-phase, Z-score
3 · Multi-scale CNNFine k=7 + coarse k=31 → 46 × 128
4 · BiLSTM + attentionLogits (5) and weights αt (46)
5 · ExplainSTFT bands, peak time, narrative

Frequently asked


Why attention instead of Grad-CAM?
Attention is built into the model, and the report validates it with deletion tests: masking the top-3 attended tokens drops true-class probability by 0.485, versus 0.061 for random masking.


What data was used?
A synthetic AASM-style generator with pink noise, spindles, K-complexes, delta waves and sawtooth waves. Validating on Sleep-EDF and SHHS is listed as future work.


What is planned next?
Public clinical databases, multi-modal fusion (airflow, SpO₂, ECG), cross-epoch transformers and INT8 edge deployment.
